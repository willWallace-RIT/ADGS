Adaptive Diegetic Governance System (ADGS)

Repository Overview

Repo Name: adaptive-diegetic-governance

The Adaptive Diegetic Governance System (ADGS) is an open research framework for modeling, forecasting, and analyzing the societal impact of large-scale conceptual exploration (diegetic exploration), institutional critique, and systemic reinterpretation.

It is not a control system. It is a decision-support and simulation framework for understanding tradeoffs between:

Intellectual exploration

Economic stability

Institutional resilience

Social coordination



---

Core Purpose

Modern societies continuously generate recursive critique of their own systems:

Economics

Governance

Identity frameworks

Labor structures

Education systems

Technological infrastructure


While this is necessary for progress, uncontrolled or poorly timed destabilization can produce:

Economic volatility

Institutional trust decay

Psychological overload

Coordination failure


At the same time, suppressing critique entirely creates stagnation and systemic harm.

ADGS exists to model this tension.


---

Key Concept

Every act of deep systemic critique has:

Local benefit (insight, correction, innovation)

Global cost (coordination disruption, trust shifts, ripple effects)


ADGS formalizes:

When critique is beneficial

When critique should be staged or delayed

When stabilization infrastructure is required

How downstream effects propagate through systems

Ethical Constraints

ADGS explicitly rejects:

Automated ideological enforcement

Censorship of academic inquiry

Centralized control of thought systems


It is designed for:

Analysis

Forecasting

Transparency

Decision support


Human oversight is required for real-world application.


---

Intended Use Cases

Academic research into systemic stability

Policy simulation environments

Economic impact forecasting of intellectual movements

Institutional resilience analysis

Risk assessment of large-scale conceptual shifts



---

Non-Goals

Not a governance replacement system

Not an enforcement mechanism

Not a censorship tool



---

Status

Early-stage conceptual framework. No implementation exists yet. Focus areas:

Taxonomy formalization

Metric definition

Simulation prototyping
